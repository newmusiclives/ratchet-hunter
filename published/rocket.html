The Rocket: S&P upside with a $2,000 loss limit

Each year: keep the $8,000 floor (it only ratchets up) in T-bills, spend up to the rest of the risk budget (at most 20% of the account) on a 1-year SPY call option, collect what it pays, raise the floor by half of any profit. Replayed on every month since 1994 with real SPY, VIX and T-bill data up to 2026-11-10. Model results, not real trades.

How the options are priced

Black-Scholes, 1-year expiry. Volatility = that day's VIX + 1 point, a little lower for higher strikes (S&P skew). Interest = that day's 13-week T-bill. Dividends = SPY's trailing yield. Plus 2% on every premium for the bid/ask spread. Real option prices differ from any model; check live quotes before trading. The S&P line includes reinvested dividends. The Hunter's deal profits are not included here; they come on top of the T-bill interest.
